# JMod

## Joint modeling of mass spectra by [McDonnell et al, 2025][JMod_Preprint]
 * **JMod is developed at Parallel Squared Technology Institute: [parallelsq.org/JMod](https://www.parallelsq.org/JMod)**
 * Code available at: [github.com/ParallelSquared/timePlex](https://github.com/ParallelSquared/timePlex)


&nbsp;



Highly parallel data acquisition generates complex mass spectra. To enhance the analysis of such complex spectra, JMod uses joint modeling. It uses the intrinsic structure in the spectra and explicitly models overlapping peaks as linear superpositions of their components. By supporting enhanced decoding of DIA spectra multiplexed in the mass and time domains, JMod provides an open and flexible software for increasing the throughput of sensitive proteomics. Reported by [McDonnell et al., 2025](https://slavovlab.net/PTI-Publications/2025_McDonnell_JMod.pdf).
