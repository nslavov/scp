* Code available at: [github.com/SlavovLab/PBMC_covariation](https://github.com/SlavovLab/PBMC_covariation)



### Methods used:
* Single-cell proteomics: [plexDIA](https://scp.slavovlab.net/plexDIA)
* Sample preparation: [nPOP](https://scp.slavovlab.net/nPOP)
* Single-cell transcriptomics: [Smart-seq3xpress](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-022-01311-4)
* Data analysis package: [QuantQC](QuantQC)
